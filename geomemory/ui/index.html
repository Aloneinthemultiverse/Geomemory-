What is this, in simple words?

Think of a diary for a city. Every event (a taxi pickup, a sensor alarm, a satellite photo) is written down with where, when and who reported it.

An AI assistant can then ask the diary questions like "what happened near here last night?" and get a correct answer instantly, with proof, instead of guessing.

The hard parts it solves: searching millions of events by place and time fast, deciding whom to believe when sources disagree, and not losing data when a computer crashes.
